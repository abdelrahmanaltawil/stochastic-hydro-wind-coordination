\begin{frame}{Research question and scope}
\begin{itemize}
\item How does jointly scheduling water pumps, photovoltaic generation, battery storage, and grid exchange affect daily procurement cost?
\item A single operator coordinates hourly decisions with known demand, generation availability, and tariffs.
\item The model respects approximate hydraulic and electrical-network constraints and replenishes initial water and battery reserves at the horizon end.
\item The implemented study concerns water distribution and electricity consumption. Stochastic wind--hydropower scheduling and decentralized price coordination are future extensions.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}{Method and evidence}
\begin{itemize}
\item Formulate a mixed-integer linear program with piecewise hydraulic curves and linearized feeder constraints.
\item Couple each pump's binary status to a calibrated fixed electrical load at its assigned bus.
\item Compare flexible and fixed pumping under identical tariffs, assets, and terminal conditions.
\item Report solver status, cost, schedules, service limits, and inventory residuals from reproducible experiments.
\item Distinguish feasibility of the optimization approximation from nonlinear replay of optimized controls and from field validation.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
